A companion that lives on your desktop.

Remembers you, talks back, and stays on your machine.

Pre-release · macOS & Linux · MIT · Bring your own agent

How it feels

Remembers you

Facts, moments, and a mood that has a cause. Your companion builds a relationship over time, not just a chat log.

Speaks and reacts

A Live2D or VRM avatar with lip sync, voice in and out. Presence you can see and hear, not just read.

Stays on your machine

Memory, persona, and relationship are local files. Nothing leaves your desktop unless you choose to send it.

On your desktop

Full window when you want to talk. Mini mode when you just want them nearby.

Their mind is your agent

Meuxe owns presence: persona, memory, voice, and avatar. Chat runs through the ACP agent you already use: Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, or any ACP agent.

macOS Apple Silicon

Download for Mac

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Meuxe into Applications.
  2. Clear the quarantine flag once (builds aren’t notarized): xattr -cr /Applications/Meuxe.app
  3. Open Meuxe from Applications or Spotlight.

Linux Debian / Ubuntu

Download .deb

Download .AppImage

  1. Install the .deb (pulls WebKitGTK 4.1 and AppIndicator): sudo apt install ./Meuxe_*.deb
  2. Or make the .AppImage executable and run it.
